### Teachings & Tradition
- Based on the teachings of Swami Sivananda
- The 4 paths of Yoga: Karma Yoga (service), Bhakti Yoga (devotion), Raja Yoga (meditation), Jnana Yoga (wisdom)
- Advaita Vedanta Philosophy
- Ayurveda, the science of Life
- Classical Hatha Yoga
- Inspired by: Swami Sivananda, Neem Karoli Baba, Ramana Maharishi, Amma, Dharma Mittra, Swami Vivekananda, Baba Ram Dass

### What They Offer
- Yoga classes (Hatha Yoga, Vinyasa)
- Kirtan nights (mantra chanting)
- Meditation sessions
- Satsang (guided meditation, chanting, spiritual discourse)
- Yoga teacher training (Profesorado de Yoga — 200 hours, 5 weeks)
- Bhakti Yoga immersion courses
- Yoga philosophy and Vedanta study
- Harmonium lessons
- Online courses
